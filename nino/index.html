The lamp at midnight

You are 丁, the yin Fire: the candle, the lamp in the window and the star you steer by when the sun has gone.

Where the yang Fire is the sun, which lights everything at once and asks nothing, the yin Fire is the small flame that has to be tended. It gives warmth to whoever is near enough and light to whoever is lost. The classics call it gentle outside and bright within, a fire that looks modest and can outlast the night.

You were born in the month of the Pig, at the opening of winter, and in the hour of the Ox, the darkest stretch of the night. Almost no chart places a flame so deep in the cold. That is the first thing to understand about you: your light was never borrowed from the season. It was kept alight by care, and it has always meant the most to the people sitting in the dark around it.

The old counsel for 丁 in winter is to feed the lamp with 甲 Wood, and your chart keeps that tree hidden inside the Pig of your month, the season itself holding fuel in reserve. Beneath you sits the Snake, where your fire has its full strength 帝旺. Whatever the weather, you have always had a hearth of your own to stand on.

A winter flame keeps well by staying warm, moving gently and resting before it is spent.

The triangle of thanks

寅巳申 · 无恩之刑

When you, Jane and Anne are in one room, the branches of your three charts complete a triangle that the classics call, a little bluntly, the punishment of ingratitude: your Snake, Jane's Tiger and the Monkeys that both your daughters share. It describes care flowing in every direction and thanks that somehow fails to complete the circle. Each of you gives in her own way, and each of you can feel unseen by the other two.

It is one of the most common shapes in loving families, and its remedy is the plainest one there is: say it aloud. Thank each other for the specific thing, the meal or the trip or the phone call, rather than trusting that it is known. Between three people who love each other this much, the words cost nothing and complete the circle.

Care in every direction · thanks said aloud
